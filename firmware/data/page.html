Flight details

For the aircraft you tap on the radar: take-off and arrival time, the flown track and a photo. Times and track come from FlightAware (your AeroAPI key, from flightaware.com/aeroapi/portal; FlightAware bills per lookup, a few for each aircraft you select, at most 60 an hour here). Photos come from Planespotters and need a contact e-mail address.

SD card log

One CSV per day (UTC) and a system log in /adsb on the card. The oldest files are deleted when less than 10 % is free.

Country and time zone follow the location unless you pick one here. The country sets the default units. Place data: GeoNames (CC-BY 4.0).

Wi-Fi

If the new network does not work, the radar goes back to the old one by itself. While it switches, this page may stop responding: reconnect your phone to your home Wi-Fi and open http://radar.local (or the address on the radar's screen).

Firmware

The radar installs into its spare slot and restarts. A new version must run for a minute with live data to be kept; if it fails to start, the radar goes back to the old one by itself. Do not switch it off while updating.
